Our Services

TV Mounting

We provide professional TV mounting services for any wall surface including drywall, brick, concrete, or above fireplaces. Our team ensures all wires are neatly concealed, offers installation of soundbars and floating shelves, and sets up smart remotes and connected devices for seamless control.

Structured Network Wiring

Our certified techs install Cat5e, Cat6, and coaxial cabling to ensure high-speed, low-latency networks throughout your home or office. We terminate jacks and patch panels, install racks and switches, and perform full network diagnostics to guarantee optimal performance.

Camera Systems

We install indoor and outdoor surveillance systems with HD/4K cameras, DVR or NVR recording, motion alerts, and mobile access. Whether you're securing a small home or a large commercial property, we tailor coverage and placement for maximum visibility and deterrence.

Home Automation

From smart lighting and voice-controlled assistants to smart thermostats, locks, and doorbells, we make your home smarter and more efficient. We integrate platforms like Alexa, Google Home, Ring, and Nest for full control and scheduling via your mobile device.
